1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Registration and account details, such as name, email address, and username.
  • Social account identifiers, profile details, page details, and channel details made available through permissions you authorize.
  • Content you choose to create, upload, schedule, or publish, such as captions, images, videos, and hashtags.
  • Publishing status, scheduled dates, and authorized comments, engagement, or analytics data.
  • Technical information such as browser, device, log, and diagnostic information.

We aim to request and access only the information needed for the features you choose to use.

2. Social Media Integrations

You may choose to connect accounts from Facebook, Instagram, X (formerly Twitter), and YouTube. Connecting an account requires authorization through the relevant platform. The information available to us depends on the platform, the permissions granted, and the APIs and features currently supported.

Facebook and Instagram

Where configured and authorized, Meta services may allow the application to retrieve account or Page details, publish content, and access supported comments, engagement, or insights. The exact capabilities depend on the permissions and account types supported by Meta.

X

Where configured and authorized, the X API may allow supported account information retrieval, content publishing, and other enabled features.

YouTube

Where configured and authorized, Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API may allow supported channel information retrieval and video-management features.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and administer application accounts.
  • To authenticate users and connect social media accounts.
  • To create, schedule, and publish content when you request those actions.
  • To display authorized account information, publishing history, and available analytics.
  • To provide support, maintain the application, diagnose errors, and improve security.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information we process. Depending on the deployed system, safeguards may include HTTPS, access controls, restricted administrative access, secure credential handling, logging, and security maintenance. Access tokens and secrets should be stored securely on the server and must not be exposed in browser-side code.

No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Do not publish this policy's security descriptions unless they accurately reflect the controls implemented in your application.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to a social media platform when needed to carry out an action you authorize, such as connecting an account or publishing content. We may use hosting, infrastructure, and support providers to operate the application, subject to appropriate contractual and security safeguards. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to provide the service, resolve disputes, maintain security, and meet legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and how you use the application.

7. Disconnecting Social Accounts

You can disconnect a social account through the application's account settings where that feature is available. You may also revoke access through the relevant platform's settings. Disconnecting an account prevents future access using the revoked authorization, subject to the platform's token and revocation behavior. Disconnecting does not necessarily delete information already stored by the application; submit a deletion request for that purpose.
