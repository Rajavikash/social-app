Instructions for requesting deletion of your information

How to Request Data Deletion

If you use the Social Media Management Application and want us to delete personal information associated with your application account or connected social media accounts, follow these steps:

  1. If you can access the application, disconnect the relevant Facebook, Instagram, X, or YouTube account in the application's account or integrations settings, where available.
  2. Send a deletion request to the privacy contact email below. Use the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your application account and identify the connected social platform(s) involved. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other login credentials.
  4. We may contact you to verify your request before processing it.

What Happens Next?

We will review your request and delete or anonymize the personal information covered by the request where appropriate. We may retain limited information when required or permitted by applicable law, for security, or to resolve legal claims. We will communicate with you if additional information is needed or if any information cannot be deleted.

Revoking Platform Access

You can also revoke our application's access directly through the settings provided by the relevant social media platform. Revoking platform access helps prevent future access through that authorization. It does not automatically delete information already stored by our application; please also send us a deletion request.
